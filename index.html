Mesh devices

Types

  • Bare Module: No case and not usable out of the box
  • Portable: Case and usable out of the box with a smartphone
  • Handheld: Case and usable out of the box standalone (additional battery may be required, e.g. for the SenseCAP Indicator)
  • Fixed: Designed as a fixed node
  • Gateway: Designed as a gateway with another protocol
  • Bare Module/Portable: Depends on the options chosen at purchase

Other remarks

  • You can select several options in the dropdown lists, an OR operator will be applied between them. Nothing selected = do not filter on this column.
  • Battery life only present if independently tested (minimum value)
  • Solar only if option present on manufacturer's website
  • Prices are in USD from manufacturer's websites
  • dBm present only if different from standard LoRa chip power, as advertised by manufacturers
  • Firmwares are from official GitHub repositories and flashers, manufacturer firmwares excluded
